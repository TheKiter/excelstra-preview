Membership

Private Advisory Membership: Coordinated Firm Stewardship.

One picture, brought together as one. Because no two lives or enterprises are alike, your engagement is shaped around your situation, your priorities, and your plan.

01

The Partnership

A Direct Partnership Where Your Life and Balance Sheet Are Held as One.

Membership is an enduring twelve-month partnership. As tax laws evolve, industries shift, and your operating business reaches new milestones, our multidisciplinary team calibrates your strategy alongside your existing advisors.

You lead the business and set the vision for your life and family. Excelstra provides the integration, technical specialists, and steady follow-through that keep every part of your plan moving together.

We lift the weight of inter-advisor coordination off your shoulders, replacing fragmented advice with one clear plan.

02

Engagements

Your Engagement Is Designed to Fit You

Where you stand today, what you are building toward, and the complexity of your enterprise determine the level at which we work together. Each engagement opens with a clear read of your whole picture, then matches the depth of our coordination to your specific needs.

The Fullest Partnership

Diamond

For the founder or family steward requiring comprehensive family-office coordination, multi-entity trust governance, and enterprise transition planning 24 to 60 months ahead of a liquidity event.

Built for: established businesses and their owners focused on tax stewardship, valuation growth, and a transition designed on their own terms.
The arc: a twelve-month engagement at our fullest depth, with up to thirty private strategy sessions. We carry the key partner facilitation and in-scope preparation, design across the entire architecture, and hold the plan steady through each move of the year. The firm, beside you.
The Full Design

Gold

For the established business owner requiring active multidisciplinary coordination across corporate entities, capital allocation, and proactive statutory tax stewardship throughout the operating year.

Built for: established business owners focused on keeping more with integrity, growing the enterprise, and building long-term family standing.
The arc: a twelve-month engagement with up to sixteen private strategy sessions. We coordinate the partner facilitation and preparation behind the strategies we put in motion, with access to private opportunities and a standing invitation to the Excelstra roundtable.

Long-Term Partnership

Continuance · Beyond the Initial Year

Wealth building evolves as your business and family grow. After your foundational twelve-month architecture is in place, Continuance keeps the Excelstra team beside you across the years ahead.

03

Qualification

Who Belongs

Membership is for self-made business owners with three to fifty million in company revenue or investable assets, or households well into the high six figures, who are ready to stop assembling their own strategy and start commanding one delivered by a unified team.

Where you begin is a conversation. We shape the engagement around your situation and your plan.
