Members, Not Clients

Built around our members, designed around your vision.

At Excelstra, we welcome members rather than taking on transactional engagements. Each partnership opens with a clear read of your full picture across tax, wealth, and transition, matching the depth of orchestration to the life you are building.

Why We Filter By Revenue & Income

Unlike traditional firms that filter by investable assets or net worth, Excelstra welcomes self-made business owners with $3M to $50M+ in annual company revenue and executives, surgeons, and physicians with $500K+ in annual household income.

Engagement Altitudes

Two Foundational Engagements & Enduring Continuance

Where you stand today, what you are building toward, and the complexity of your enterprise determine the altitude at which we work together. Each engagement is retained through a transparent engagement fee discussed privately during your consultation.

Altitude I · Gold
The Full Design

Gold

12-Month Engagement Up to 16 Strategy Sessions

For the established business owner or executive requiring active multidisciplinary orchestration across corporate entities, Strategic Tax Stewardship, and curated Wealth Amplification throughout the operating year.

Built for: Self-made business owners ($3M to $50M+ revenue, often without a CFO) and leaders focused on keeping more with integrity, scaling enterprise value, and building enduring wealth.

The Arc: We carry more of the implementation preparation and partner facilitation for you, coordinating directly with your CPA and counsel, with curated access to independent opportunities and the Excelstra peer roundtable.

Diamond
