In Her Voice

Executive Background & Advisory Ethos

I spent more than a decade in the rooms where Fortune 500 strategy gets made, advising the kind of leadership most builders rarely get near. Deloitte, EY, Booz&Co. Rooms at Coca-Cola, Bank of America, American Express. I have been recognized in Marquis Who's Who.

In those rooms I saw how the largest fortunes are actually run. A team of specialists works from one design, each the master of a single part, all answering to the same plan.

When I sat with private business owners and family founders, I saw a very different reality. These entrepreneurs had capable advisors across tax, investments, and law, yet each worked in a separate room without seeing the full life and business behind the balance sheet.

The founder spent their evenings acting as the bridge between disconnected recommendations, carrying both the work of the business and the weight of holding the plan together.

Origin

Why Excelstra Was Formed

Excelstra was formed to bring board-level coordination and human care to private business owners. We established a firm of specialists across statutory tax stewardship, capital allocation, entity structuring, and legacy exit planning, accountable to one another and to the entirety of your picture.

Every season of building brings contrast, and those experiences teach us what matters most. We know that your business and your wealth grow in step with your personal clarity. I lead the firm with direct personal stewardship: listening to who you are first, setting the strategic standard, and ensuring you are seen, heard, and held at every step.
