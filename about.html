EUNICIA PERET · MBA FOUNDER & CEO · EXCELSTRA
A Note from Eunicia

From institutional strategy to deeply human stewardship.

20+ Years Advising Complex Enterprises & Ultra-High-Net-Worth Families

"Earlier in my career inside Deloitte, Ernst & Young, and Booz & Company, I saw how the largest institutions on earth made decisions. They never allowed tax, legal, capital allocation, and executive purpose to sit in disconnected silos. Every decision was modeled for its full ripple effect.

Yet when I sat privately with seven- and eight-figure founders, I saw the exact opposite. I saw extraordinary people who were overgiving to their employees, their clients, and everyone around them, while carrying a quiet terror of making one wrong move and losing what they built. Many had already been burned by a CPA, an attorney, or a financial advisor who imposed a cookie-cutter strategy without ever asking what hard knocks were happening inside the business.

We need contrast for wisdom. Those painful experiences taught us what we refuse to tolerate. I created Excelstra so that you never again have to choose between institutional-grade financial precision and being genuinely seen, heard, and held as a human being. Your business and your wealth can only expand as far as your personal development does. Here, we honor both."
