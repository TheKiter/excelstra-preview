In Her Voice

Executive Background & Advisory Ethos

I spent more than a decade in the rooms where Fortune 500 strategy gets made, advising the kind of leadership most builders rarely get near. Deloitte, EY, Booz&Co. Rooms at Coca-Cola, Bank of America, American Express. I have been recognized in Marquis Who's Who.

In those rooms I saw how the largest fortunes are actually run. A team of specialists works from one design, each the master of a single part, all answering to the same plan.

When I observed the private business owners and family founders driving economic growth, I recognized a profound structural gap. These entrepreneurs possessed exceptional advisors: a diligent CPA, a reputable wealth manager, a trusted attorney. Yet none of these specialists communicated with one another.

The founder spent their evenings acting as the de facto coordinator, translating disjointed advice while thousands in tax drag leaked between the cracks.

Why Excelstra Was Formed

Excelstra was formed to bring institutional board-level coordination to private business owners. We established a firm of specialists across statutory tax architecture, capital allocation, entity structuring, and dynastic succession, accountable to one another and to the entirety of your balance sheet.

I lead the firm with direct personal stewardship: setting the strategic standard, guiding the architecture, and holding the principal relationship. Our specialist bench executes with rigorous technical depth. The result is a synchronized advisory firm that protects your capital, amplifies your growth, and permanently secures your family legacy.
