Strategic Due Diligence

Five Questions Worth Asking About Your Whole Picture

The quality of a wealth strategy begins with the quality of the questions behind it. Bring these five questions to your next planning review.

01
01: Who connects your CPA, investment advisor, and estate attorney around one shared plan?
When each specialist works from the same understanding of your life and enterprise, tax strategies support investment timing and long-term transition goals stay in step with current cash flow.
02
02: How is your statutory tax position designed to create capital for your next move?
When tax stewardship is planned years ahead alongside entity structure and capital allocation, lawful tax savings can be systematically redeployed into the business or family balance sheet.
03
03: Is your transition or exit architecture being prepared 24 to 60 months in advance?
A transaction is a single day; enduring family wealth is built well before a sale. Early design strengthens valuation, positions equity inside appropriate trusts, and prepares your next chapter on your terms.
04
04: If you stepped back from daily operations tomorrow, is your family governance clearly documented?
Enduring stewardship rests on clear entity charters, protective trusts, and a coordinated team that knows the whole picture and can guide your family with continuity.
05
05: How does your current financial strategy support your personal vitality and the life you want to live now?
Every decision creates a ripple. Wealth serves its highest purpose when it supports your health, frees your energy for the work you love, and deepens connection with the people who matter most.
