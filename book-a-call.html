03 / Five Diagnostic Questions for Your Current Advisors

How to tell if you are receiving
skipping stones or sinking rocks.

If you are wondering whether your current CPA, estate attorney, or wealth advisor is truly seeing your whole picture, bring these five questions to your next review meeting.

Diagnostic Question 01

"Before you recommend this strategy, how does it serve what I actually want my life and business to look like in three years?"

If an advisor cannot articulate your personal relevance, your exhaustion points, and what you are trying to stop doing inside your company, they are selling a template rather than architecting your life.

Diagnostic Question 02

"When was the last time you sat in the same room with my CPA, my corporate attorney, and my estate counsel without me having to translate?"

When advisors operate in silos, you become the unpaid project manager carrying the emotional and technical risk of their contradictions.

Diagnostic Question 03

"What are the second- and third-order ripple effects of this tax or entity move on my liquidity and exit optionality?"

Many aggressive tax tactics save money in April but trap capital or impairs enterprise valuation when industry consolidation or a buyout opportunity arrives.

Diagnostic Question 04

"How are you compensated on the investments or structures you propose, and who audits your recommendations on my behalf?"

True peace of mind requires knowing that your strategic architect sits exclusively on your side of the table with zero hidden product agenda.

Diagnostic Question 05

"How does this plan prepare my spouse and children for the frequency and responsibility of the wealth we are building?"

Documents alone do not create family harmony. A complete wealth architecture prepares the people you love to steward both the capital and the values behind it.
