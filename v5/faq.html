Frequently Asked Questions: Advisory Architecture & Operations

Clear, unvarnished insight into how Excelstra coordinates multidisciplinary wealth for enterprise founders.

Is Excelstra one advisor or a firm?

Excelstra is an institutional advisory firm led by Eunicia Peret. Our multidisciplinary bench coordinates statutory tax architecture, capital allocation, corporate structure, and exit transition under a unified command layer. You engage an entire strategic firm rather than fractional advisor hours.

Do you replace my CPA, advisor, or attorney?

No. We actively collaborate alongside your trusted CPA, wealth manager, and corporate counsel. Excelstra provides the overarching master blueprint that synchronizes their individual disciplines, closing the uncoordinated gaps that cause tax drag and strategic friction.

What does wealth integration actually mean?

Wealth integration is the deliberate alignment of statutory tax planning, capital allocation, and legacy governance under a single balance sheet strategy. We evaluate your entire enterprise architecture before modifying individual structures, uncovering multi-million-dollar efficiencies that single-discipline advisors cannot see in isolation.

Who is Excelstra for?

Excelstra is built exclusively for established business founders, family enterprise principals, and creators generating $3M to $50M+ in enterprise value who demand coordinated advisory command across corporate and family balance sheets.

How is my engagement decided?

By your situation and your plan. Because one size does not fit all, we match the depth of the work to where you are and what you are building toward, and we design the right engagement together in the conversation.

How do I begin?

Two ways. Run the wealth calculator for a private, two-minute view of your own picture, or book a call and speak with the team directly. Most members begin with the calculator and move to a conversation when they are ready.

What happens on the first call?

A sixty-minute conversation rather than a pitch. We listen first, then show you what is possible for your picture. You leave clearer either way, whether or not you choose to work with us.

What does membership cost?

Investment depends on the design your picture calls for, so it is discussed in a private conversation rather than listed. The tiers are described by what a member receives.
