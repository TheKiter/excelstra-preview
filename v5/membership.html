The Partnership

A Direct Partnership in Strategic Wealth Governance.

Membership is not a transactional consultation; it is an enduring advisory partnership. As statutory tax laws evolve, markets shift, and your operating business reaches new liquidity milestones, our multidisciplinary team actively recalibrates your architecture.

You command the operating enterprise and strategic vision. Excelstra provides the institutional design, technical disciplines, and execution cadence that insulate your wealth.

03

Engagements

Your Engagement Is Designed to Fit You

No two pictures are alike, so no two engagements are. Where you stand today, what you are building toward, and the plan it calls for decide the altitude at which we work together. Three engagements meet our members where they are at. Each opens the same way, with a clear read of your whole picture, then matches the depth of the work to your specific needs.

The Fullest Partnership

Diamond

For the principal requiring comprehensive family office coordination, dynastic trust governance, and enterprise exit engineering 24 to 60 months prior to liquidity.

Built for: established businesses and their owners focused on tax, growth, valuation, and a transition designed on their own terms.
The arc: a twelve-month engagement at the fullest level, with up to thirty private strategy sessions. We carry the key partner facilitation and the in-scope preparation, design across the whole architecture, and hold the plan steady through each move of the year. The firm, beside you.
The Full Design

Gold

For the established owner requiring active multidisciplinary coordination across corporate entities, private market allocations, and strategic statutory tax mitigation throughout the operating calendar.

Built for: established business owners focused on lowering tax, growing the enterprise, and building long-term financial standing.
The arc: a twelve-month engagement with up to sixteen private strategy sessions. We carry more of the work for you, coordinating most of the partner facilitation and the preparation behind the strategies we put in motion, with access to opportunities most owners rarely see and a standing invitation to the Excelstra roundtable.
The Foundation

Copper

For the leader bringing their picture together for the first time. A focused engagement that sets a strong baseline and a personal strategy across tax, portfolio, and the income that carries you forward.

Built for: individuals, families, and owners of growing or earlier-stage businesses, ready to design with intention.
The arc: a six-month engagement with up to six private strategy sessions. We map your whole picture, build your personal plan, identify the top levers and changes that set the rest in motion, and introduce the strategic support partners your plan calls for. You leave with a clear design and the first moves underway.
