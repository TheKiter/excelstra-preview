Why a Team Sees What One Advisor Cannot

Tax law, portfolio strategy, legal structure, and exit design are four deep disciplines, and the leaders who keep the most wealth long ago stopped asking one gifted person to hold all four. They engage a firm instead, where each specialist is the best at their part and a single plan keeps those parts in step.
